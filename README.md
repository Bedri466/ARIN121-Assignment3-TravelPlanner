# ARIN121 Assignment 3 — Travel Planner

This is my travel planner from Assignment 2, extended for Assignment 3. It runs in Google AI Studio Playground.

## What it can do

- **Build me a trip** — asks for your destination, how long you're going for, who you're travelling with, and your dates, one question at a time. Then it makes a day-by-day plan.
- **Find my travel personality** — asks three questions: city or nature, then a follow-up depending on your answer (beach or mountains, or street food or sit-down meals), then calm or busy. After that it sums up your travel style and uses it when it builds your plan.

## How to use it

Just ask something general like "I need help with my trip" and the menu will show up:

1. Build me a trip
2. Find my travel personality

You can pick a number, say the option name, or just describe what you want in your own words. You can also switch activities partway through and it will keep the trip details you already gave it.
